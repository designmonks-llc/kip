          Questions

          Good to know.

          Yes. Kip’s transcription runs on your Mac instead of on our servers, so there’s nothing for us to pay for per meeting, and nothing to charge you.

          Kip never joins the call, so no bot shows up. It’s still good manners to say you’re taking notes, and Kip gives you a one-line message to paste in the chat.

          Any of them: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Webex and more. Kip floats on top and listens to your microphone and the call’s sound on your Mac.

          The browser version is the quickest way to try Kip and works well for calls in another browser tab. The Mac app can also listen to desktop apps like Zoom and Teams, and floats over everything.

          Kip notices when someone speaks Bangla and switches on its own. It gets the gist well; English words mixed into Bangla sentences can come out rough. It improves with each update.

          Only on your Mac. You can copy or download any page as Markdown, and Time Machine backs Kip up along with everything else.

          Kip checks for new versions on its own and installs them quietly. You’ll see a small “restart to update” note. Your notes stay exactly where they are.
